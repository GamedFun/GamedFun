PopPlanet

Peek, remember, and match the planets — before they grow and smash together.

  • 👈 Tap two planets to peek. A matching pair goes POP!
  • 🌏 Unmatched planets keep growing — big ones glow red.
  • 💥 If two big planets touch, it's game over.
  • 🚀 Match pairs to charge up, then launch a ship and blast them Asteroids-style!

On a computer: arrow keys to fly, space to launch & shoot.
