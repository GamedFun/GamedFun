How to play
  1. Swipe anywhere on the board to aim, then release to launch — swipe farther for more power. You can't aim through the red NO-AIM zone.
  2. Charge the target! The ball must bounce the required number of times (shown as X/N on the target) before it counts. Ricochet off walls and the obstacles already on the board.
  3. Grab glowing power-ups (⚡ boost, ❄ slow, ✦ charge). Purple ◈ phase blocks blink in and out of existence — shoot while they're gone, or use them while they're solid.
